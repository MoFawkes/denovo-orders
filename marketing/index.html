What we do

CMT manufacturing for womenswear, done properly.

You supply the design and fabric; we cut, make and trim. From your tech pack or an approved sample, we deliver production runs that match the garment you signed off — style after style, repeat after repeat.

Everything happens in the UK, in our own Leicester unit, by machinists we employ directly. That means fair conditions, full visibility, no sub-contract surprises — and lead times measured in weeks, not seasons.

Tops & blouses

Trousers & skirts

Tailored and pull-on styles, including bengaline and ponte stretch.

Co-ords & sets

Matched two-pieces and loungewear, cut for consistent colour and fit.

How we work

Four steps. No guesswork.

Nothing goes into bulk until you have approved a sample you can hold in your hands. From there, every order runs through our live production tracker — so you always know exactly which stage your styles are at, without having to chase.

  1. 01

    Sampling

    Our in-house sample team develops your style from a tech pack, sketch or reference garment. We iterate until you approve — nothing is cut in bulk before sign-off.

  2. 02

    Cutting

    Fabric is checked in, relaxed and laid, then cut against the approved sample's specs and your size ratios for a clean, consistent run.

  3. 03

    Production

    Our machinists sew your order on dedicated lines. Progress is logged in our live tracking system at every stage, so your order's status is never a mystery.

  4. 04

    Quality check & delivery

    Every garment is inspected, pressed and packed to your packing list, then booked for dispatch — arriving floor-ready, on the date we agreed.

Why Denovo

"Designed in the UK" should mean made here too. Producing in Leicester keeps your supply chain short, your carbon footprint smaller, and your label honest — and it puts your production partner a train ride away, not a time zone away.

A decade of making, and counting

Over ten years of CMT manufacturing means your styles are handled by people who have seen — and solved — almost everything a garment can throw at a factory.

Fast turnaround

No shipping containers, no customs queues. UK production lets you sample quickly, react to sell-through and re-cut winners while the season is still live.

Transparent communication

One point of contact, straight answers, and live order tracking. If a delivery date moves, you hear it from us first — with a plan, not an excuse.

Ethical, and provably so

Our own premises, our own team, UK employment standards — and an open door. Buyers are welcome to visit the factory floor any time their order is running.
